# Assumptions
- bread, PB, knife are on table
- bread is sliced inside bag

# Steps
- remove twist tie
- open bread bag
- reach into bag, grab 2 pieces of bread
- lay both pieces of bread on countertop side by side
- grab jar, move near bread
- unscrew jar lid
- break the seal, to come in contact with the PB
- drop the lid
- grab knife
- scoop PB out of open jar
- with PB on knife, spread on one piece of bread
- put knife away
- put no-PB bread on top of bread with PB

# Done When
- Bread, peanut butter, Bread, all on the plate or table, in one piece with 3 layers.
